Companion Media Network

Media Partners

Our Media Partners serve as a vital bridge connecting Lưu Sắc Kim Hoàng with readers, folk art lovers, and especially young generations. Their partnership helps stories of the Kim Hoàng red painting revival reach audiences far and wide.

Media Partner

Youth+

Youth+ is a trusted platform for “SIMPLE & ACCURATE CAREER GUIDANCE AND JOB MATCHING” for young people, built on 4 core values:

  • Connection: Connecting youth with peers, mentors, and businesses.
  • Sharing: A community for exchanging and learning multidisciplinary knowledge.
  • Value: Constantly striving to create real value for young people.
  • Trust: Building a trustworthy environment for sustainable companionship with future generations.
“Youth+'s partnership means a great deal to Lưu Sắc Kim Hoàng, directly bringing the stories of those who revived Kim Hoàng paintings closer to Vietnamese youth.”
Youth Ecosystem · Guidance & Development View Post on Fanpage
Media Partner

Faculty of Communications – FPT University Hà Nội

The Lưu Sắc Kim Hoàng project is a meaningful communications initiative carried out by Multimedia Communications students at FPT University (FPTU). The Faculty's patronage is both a mark of academic excellence and a great source of pride for the team.

In FPTU's Communications Technology program, students train in a hands-on, industry-aligned environment, joining real-world projects from year one, learning from leading experts, and developing strategic thinking, multimedia creativity, data analytics, and multi-platform campaign execution.

“The Faculty of Communications at FPT University provides strong academic and media support, helping spread our tribute to folk artisans and heritage painting professionally and creatively.”
Higher Education · Communications Technology · FPTU View Post on Fanpage
Media Partner

Giải Trí Văn Hóa

Giải Trí Văn Hóa is a dedicated digital publication continuously covering standout news and insightful perspectives on culture, arts, and contemporary entertainment.

Here, readers not only stay up to date on trending events but also explore diverse topics across Events, Arts, Behind-the-Scenes, Youth, Travel, and Technology.

“Lưu Sắc Kim Hoàng believes that when human values are communicated at the right time, place, and audience, they multiply and leave lasting impressions on the community's cultural life.”
Cultural News · Arts · Youth View Post on Fanpage
Media Partner

Connect Vietnam (Tracuudi.vn)

Connect Vietnam is a pioneer in building a technology, data, and media ecosystem centered on traditional Vietnamese culture.

Driven by the desire to discover, access, and preserve traditional cultural archives, Connect Vietnam developed the digital platform Tracuudi.vn — an open cultural data portal connecting information, imagery, and archives on historical relics, traditional festivals, craft villages, artisans, and regional heritage. Together with the community, it preserves, shares, and carries forward Vietnam's cultural treasures.

“Connect Vietnam's partnership is a crucial digital bridge, bringing archives, imagery, and knowledge of Kim Hoàng red paintings to culture-loving readers nationwide.”

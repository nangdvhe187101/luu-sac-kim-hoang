Sponsor Information

The Lưu Sắc Kim Hoàng campaign sincerely thanks all Sponsors across every tier for their trust, companionship, and invaluable support. Their generosity empowers us to honor those who revived Kim Hoàng folk paintings and bring traditional vermilion strokes back into modern life.

Diamond Sponsors

Bảo tàng Hà Nội

Bảo tàng Hà Nội was established in 1982 to preserve, safeguard, and promote the iconic historical and cultural heritage of Vietnam's thousand-year-old capital.

With its distinctive inverted-pyramid architecture, in 2016 the Bảo tàng Hà Nội was named by Business Insider (USA) as one of the 36 most beautiful museums in the world. Today, its permanent galleries house over 7,000 artifacts and documents, including 6 national treasures and treasure groups, organized across 7 themes and 35 sub-themes vividly chronicling Thăng Long – Hà Nội.

“Bảo tàng Hà Nội's support as our Diamond Sponsor is a tremendous encouragement to Lưu Sắc Kim Hoàng, reflecting our shared mission to preserve, honor, and spread traditional cultural heritage to the public.”
Hà Nội · History & Culture Museum View Post on Fanpage

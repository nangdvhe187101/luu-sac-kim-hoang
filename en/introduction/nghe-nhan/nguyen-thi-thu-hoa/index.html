Introducing Our Companion Expert of “Lưu Sắc Kim Hoàng”

Dr. Nguyễn Thị Thu Hòa

LƯU SẮC KIM HOÀNG proudly introduces Dr. Nguyễn Thị Thu Hòa — Director of the Hà Nội Ceramic Museum, who has made pivotal contributions to researching and reviving Kim Hoàng folk paintings.

With decades of researching and collecting Vietnamese folk art, Dr. Thu Hòa initiated the Kim Hoàng revival project in 2015. From surviving archives and traces, she brought together scholars, artisans, and painters to verify and restore a nearly lost artistic heritage.

The journey went far beyond recreating lost prints. It involved researching surviving archives and artifacts, studying forms, pigments, paper, and techniques, and uniting specialists to restore motifs, woodblocks, and hand-coloring methods — yielding 33 woodblock prints and 19 hand-painted works while passing the craft on to local residents.

Alongside the Kim Hoàng revival, Dr. Nguyễn Thị Thu Hòa has dedicated herself to documenting and introducing Vietnam's folk painting traditions through scholarly publications, systematizing archives and deepening public appreciation of their artistic, cultural, and historical significance.

Dr. Thu Hòa's companionship is a cornerstone of the story LƯU SẮC KIM HOÀNG seeks to share — for behind the return of a heritage craft stand the tireless individuals who preserved its techniques and kept Kim Hoàng colors alive today.

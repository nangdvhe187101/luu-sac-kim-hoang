About

LƯU SẮC KIM HOÀNG

TOUCH THE VERMILION · PRESERVE THE PEOPLE'S MARK

Lưu Sắc Kim Hoàng is a communication campaign honoring the journey of reviving Kim Hoàng folk paintings, a traditional painting genre with a strong artistic and cultural imprint of Hà Nội.

Originating in the second half of the 18th century in Kim Hoàng village, Hoài Đức, Hà Nội, Kim Hoàng paintings were renowned for their signature vermilion-red paper, delicate woodblock carvings, and expressive color palette. Yet amid historical upheavals and the passage of time, this folk tradition nearly faded into oblivion.

Born from that story, Lưu Sắc Kim Hoàng sets out to chronicle the revival journey while recognizing and honoring the artisans, researchers, and individuals who devoted themselves to safeguarding Kim Hoàng art. Through storytelling, exhibitions, and hands-on experiences, the campaign invites the public to discover the beauty of Kim Hoàng paintings and rekindle a lasting love for traditional Vietnamese folk art.

Campaign Objective

VISION

Bringing Kim Hoàng folk paintings closer to the public; serving as a bridge between heritage custodians and folk art enthusiasts, fostering appreciation, contemporary creativity, and the continuity of Kim Hoàng artistic values in modern life.

Action

MISSION

  • Honor the revival journey: Share the story of restoring Kim Hoàng folk paintings and the timeless artistic values preserved across generations.
  • Express gratitude: Pay tribute to the artisans, researchers, and dedicated individuals who poured their hearts and efforts into reviving and preserving this craft.
  • Connect with the public: Create opportunities for everyone to learn, explore, and directly experience the art of Kim Hoàng folk painting.
  • Continue the legacy: Inspire a love for traditional art and encourage younger generations to care for, preserve, and share Kim Hoàng heritage in contemporary life.
